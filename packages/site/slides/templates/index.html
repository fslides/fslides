Templates

Designed starting points for fslides create and fslides scaffold. Every slide below is live — the real HTML, scaled down.

Charcoal

Dark, confident, data-forward — dot-grid charcoal with a teal accent. Built for numbers.

$ fslides scaffold my-deck --template charcoal

Paper

Light editorial — warm paper, serif display type, one burnt-orange accent. For narrative talks.

$ fslides scaffold my-deck --template paper

Minimal

The default — one blank-canvas title slide, zero opinions. Omit --template to get it.

$ fslides scaffold my-deck
